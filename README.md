# DealLitmus

Real estate deal analyzer for rentals, BRRRRs and fix-and-flips. Runs in your browser: no account,
and your numbers stay on your device.

**Use it: https://carlmaccari-code.github.io/deallitmus/**

- [Open the analyzer](https://carlmaccari-code.github.io/deallitmus/app/)
- [Free calculators](https://carlmaccari-code.github.io/deallitmus/calculators/): rental cash flow,
  BRRRR, fix and flip, cap rate, DSCR, cash-on-cash, house hacking, short-term rental and more

This repository holds the published site.
