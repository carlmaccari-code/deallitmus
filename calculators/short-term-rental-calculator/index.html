Short-term rental calculator

Nightly rate times occupancy looks great until the fees, the furniture and the mortgage arrive. See what is left.

How short-term rental cash flow is calculated

  1. Revenue = nightly rate × nights in the month × occupancy.
  2. Subtract fees that scale with revenue: the booking platform’s cut and any co-host or property manager.
  3. Subtract operating costs: property taxes, insurance, utilities, internet, supplies, lawn and snow, software.
  4. Subtract the mortgage. What is left is cash flow.

Break-even occupancy is the number to watch

It is the share of nights you must book just to cover your costs. If your break-even is 56% and the market averages 60%, one slow season or one new competitor down the street erases the profit. A wide gap between expected and break-even occupancy is what makes a short-term rental resilient.

Costs that long-term landlords do not have

  • Furnishing. Furniture, linens, kitchenware and photos are real cash invested, and they wear out.
  • Utilities. You pay all of them, all year.
  • Turnover. Cleaning between every stay. If guests pay a cleaning fee that covers it, leave it out of both revenue and costs.
  • Lodging taxes and permits. Many cities tax, license, cap or ban short-term rentals. Confirm the rules before you buy, and check that the property also works as a long-term rental in case they change.

Questions

What occupancy rate should I assume?

Use data for your specific market and property type rather than a national average, and be conservative in the first year while the listing builds reviews. Many markets average between 50% and 65%, with strong seasonal swings.

How much do short-term rental managers charge?

Full-service managers commonly charge 15% to 25% of revenue, on top of the booking platform’s own fee. Self-managing saves that money in exchange for your time.

Is a short-term rental better than a long-term rental?

It can earn more, but with more work, more variable income and more regulatory risk. Run the same property both ways. If it only works as a short-term rental, you are depending on rules and demand that can change.
