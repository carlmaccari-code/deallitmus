House hacking calculator

Live in one unit, rent out the rest. See what your housing actually costs once the tenants pay their share.

How house hacking math works

Net housing cost = full monthly payment + reserves − rent collected

The full monthly payment is principal, interest, taxes, insurance and any mortgage insurance. Reserves are what you set aside from the rent for repairs and the months a unit sits empty. What remains after your tenants pay is what it costs you to live there.

For example, if the full payment is about $2,960 and the other unit rents for $1,700, you are living there for roughly $1,430 a month after reserves, in a property you own.

Why it works

Owner-occupied loans allow much smaller down payments than investment loans, often 3.5% to 5% instead of 20% to 25%, and usually carry lower rates. House hacking lets you buy a rental with owner-occupant financing, as long as you genuinely live in it. Lenders generally require you to move in and stay for a minimum period, commonly a year.

Run it as a rental too

The test of a good house hack is what happens when you move out. Before you buy, analyze the property as a full rental with every unit rented. If it cash flows then, you have bought an investment. If it only works while you live there, you have bought a discounted home, which can still be a fine decision.

Questions

Can I count the rental income to qualify for the loan?

Often, yes. Many loan programs let you count a portion of the expected rent from the other units, commonly 75%. The rules differ by program and lender, so ask before you make an offer.

What kinds of property work for house hacking?

Duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes are the classic choice. Single-family homes with rented rooms, a basement apartment or an accessory dwelling unit also work.

Is the money I save the same as cash flow?

Not exactly. Most house hacks reduce your housing cost rather than produce income. The comparison that matters is your net cost against what you would otherwise pay in rent, which this calculator shows.
