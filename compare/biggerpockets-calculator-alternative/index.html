When BiggerPockets is the better choice

The BiggerPockets hub listed ten calculators on October 5, 2026, one a downloadable spreadsheet.

  • Wholesaling, short-term rental and rehab tools. It has a Wholesaling calculator for maximum allowable offers, an Airbnb calculator for short-term rentals and a Rehab Estimator with more than 40 repair categories. DealLitmus's full deal analysis covers rental, BRRRR and fix-and-flip deals.
  • Rent estimates. The Rent Estimator estimates rent for an address from comparable nearby properties. DealLitmus has no rent estimates, sales comps or property data.
  • Reports and saved work. Reports can be shared and downloaded as a PDF with property photos, graphs and charts. A "View my reports" link indicates reports are kept with your online account.
  • More than calculators. Pro also lists Deal Finder (filters and notifications for new properties), Market Finder, discounted BPCON tickets and a forum badge. Annual and two-year plans add partner discounts and lease packages. There is also an AI assistant connector for the calculators and a free account for learning and community.

As listed on its site on October 5, 2026, Pro costs $39 per month billed monthly, $390 billed annually, or $672 billed every two years. Prices may change. Its help center says the deal calculators require Pro or an active free trial. When we checked, results on the Rental Property and BRRRR calculators were locked until you upgrade to Pro or start the trial. We did not test every calculator. The 7-day trial asks for payment details, charges $0.00 that day and converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled. Under the Terms, fees are non-refundable, except for a full refund, less any cancellation fees, if you upgrade and cancel within 24 hours without using the paid services. You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current term, and subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.

We did not verify whether BiggerPockets imports listing data or what it offers on mobile.

Where DealLitmus fits

DealLitmus is a web app that analyzes rental, BRRRR and fix-and-flip deals from numbers you enter. It launched in October 2026, is made by one independent developer and has no track record yet. The free plan includes full analysis for all three strategies, buy-box scoring, 30-year projections and charts, 17 standalone calculators, share-by-link, and backup and restore to a file. It saves up to 3 deals.

Pro costs $39 one-time and adds unlimited saved deals, PDF reports through your browser's print-to-PDF, an offer price calculator, sensitivity tables, side-by-side comparison and CSV export of the 30-year projection. Agent costs $99 one-time and includes everything in Pro plus your logo, colors, contact details and a custom disclaimer on reports. There is no subscription. A 14-day money-back guarantee applies. See pricing.

There is no account. Deals are stored in your browser on your device, are never uploaded and do not sync between devices automatically. Recovering them depends on a backup file you save.

Other limits: no listing import from Zillow, Redfin, Realtor or the MLS; no cloud sync or team collaboration; no tax or depreciation modeling (all figures are pre-tax); no commercial or large multifamily underwriting; no native iOS or Android app (it runs in the browser on phone or desktop); US dollars only.

The bottom line

$39 one-time and $39 per month are not a like-for-like comparison. BiggerPockets Pro is a membership with rent estimates, deal finding, a rehab estimator, reports kept with your account and a community. DealLitmus is a standalone calculator.

Choose BiggerPockets Pro if you want those things. Consider DealLitmus if you already have your numbers, analyze rentals, BRRRRs or flips, and want no account or subscription. Try the free rental property calculator first.
