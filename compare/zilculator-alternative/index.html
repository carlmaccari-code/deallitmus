When Zilculator is the better choice

Zilculator is a deal analysis and marketing platform; DealLitmus is a calculator. Choose Zilculator if you need any of these.

  • Property data. It loads property details from an address. Its help center says loading by MLS number is for Premium users in the US, subject to local availability.
  • Comps and rent estimates. It loads recent sales comps to build a CMA (automatically in the United States and Canada, per its help center) and shows Rentometer rent estimates with rental comps.
  • Wholesale and commercial deals. Both listed plans cover rental, fix and flip and wholesale analysis, plus commercial and multifamily properties. BRRRR uses the rental form with a refinance loan.
  • Marketing. Online and PDF reports can carry your logo and contact details and open with an AI-written summary. Reports can be shared by email or social networks, lead forms send buyer details to your inbox or CRM, and Premium adds a property inventory website.
  • Teams. Multi-user accounts are available by request, and an Agency plan with 5 seats is announced as launching soon.

It also has a side-by-side comparison tool and maximum allowable offer calculations for flips and wholesale deals; DealLitmus's side-by-side comparison and offer price calculator are in its paid Pro tier. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial (credit card required), and a subscription can be cancelled, effective at the end of the current billing period. Its pricing FAQ offers a refund by email, and its Terms of Use state a 15-day money-back guarantee.

Where DealLitmus fits

DealLitmus is for people who already have their numbers. The free plan covers full analysis of rental, BRRRR and fix-and-flip deals, buy-box scoring, 30-year projections, 17 standalone calculators, share-by-link and file backup, and saves up to 3 deals.

Paid tiers are one-time purchases. Pro ($39) adds unlimited saved deals, PDF reports through the browser's print-to-PDF, an offer price calculator, sensitivity tables, side-by-side comparison and CSV export of the 30-year projection. Agent ($99) includes everything in Pro plus your logo, colors, contact details and a custom disclaimer on reports. Both carry a 14-day money-back guarantee. See pricing.

Zilculator's subscription includes data and marketing tools that DealLitmus lacks, so the prices are not like for like.

The limits:

  • No listing import, property data, comps or rent estimates. You type in every number.
  • No account or cloud sync. Deals are stored in the browser on your device, are never uploaded, and do not sync between devices automatically. Clearing browser data can remove them unless you have saved a backup file.
  • The full deal analysis covers rental, BRRRR and fix-and-flip only; wholesale is not one of its strategies. No commercial or large multifamily underwriting, and no team collaboration.
  • Pre-tax figures only, with no tax or depreciation modeling. US dollars only.
  • No native iOS or Android app. DealLitmus runs in the browser on phone or desktop; Zilculator describes itself as a platform with a web interface.
  • No track record. DealLitmus launched in October 2026 and is made by an independent developer.

The bottom line

If you need comps, rent estimates, MLS data, wholesale marketing, commercial deals, lead capture or multi-user accounts, choose Zilculator. DealLitmus does not replace it. If you already have your numbers and want a pay-once calculator with no account, try the free rental property calculator first.
