When Mashvisor is the better choice

Mashvisor and DealLitmus are different kinds of product, and an investor could reasonably use both.

  • Data and rent estimates. Mashvisor says Lite includes long-term and Airbnb rental rate estimates, investment opportunity scores and short-term rental rules for 500+ cities, and that its data covers 95% of US markets. DealLitmus has no property data or rent estimates, so you type in every number.
  • Comps. Standard and Professional include traditional and Airbnb rental comps, and the property page shows previous sales of similar properties. DealLitmus has no sales or rental comps.
  • Finding deals. Property Finder searches up to five locations. Standard adds heatmaps, neighborhood analytics and market comparison. Professional adds foreclosure filters and multifamily listings for up to 3 requested cities. DealLitmus has no property search and does not import listings from Zillow, Redfin, Realtor or the MLS.
  • Analysis. Each Mashvisor property page has an interactive calculator that shows cap rate, cash on cash return and cash flow. It compares long-term and Airbnb rental for each property, pre-fills expenses with area averages (editable on Standard and Professional) and can analyze an unlisted property from its address and basic details.
  • Agent tools. Professional lists a CRM, client email campaigns, 60 Excel search exports per month and client-ready PDF reports. Its help center says you can add your company name and logo when you download a property report.

Choose Mashvisor if you need to find properties or markets, want rent estimates and comps, or are evaluating an Airbnb.

Where DealLitmus fits

DealLitmus is a web app that analyzes rental, BRRRR and fix-and-flip deals from numbers you enter yourself. It is an alternative only for that job, not a replacement for Mashvisor's data.

The free plan saves up to 3 deals and includes full analysis for all three strategies, buy-box scoring against your own minimums, 30-year projections and charts, 17 free standalone calculators, share-by-link, and backup and restore to a file. There is no account. Deals are stored in your browser on your device and never uploaded, so they do not sync between devices automatically and can be lost if browser data is cleared without a backup file.

Pro costs $39 one-time and adds unlimited saved deals, PDF reports through the browser's print-to-PDF, an offer price calculator, sensitivity tables, side-by-side deal comparison and CSV export of the 30-year projection. Agent costs $99 one-time and includes everything in Pro plus your logo, colors, contact details and a custom disclaimer on reports. There is no subscription and no trial of paid features, only a 14-day money-back guarantee. See pricing.

Other limits: no native iOS or Android app (it runs in the browser on phone or desktop), no tax or depreciation modeling (all figures are pre-tax), no commercial or large multifamily underwriting, no cloud sync, CRM or team collaboration, and US dollars only. DealLitmus launched in October 2026 and is made by an independent developer.

The bottom line

Mashvisor's list prices on October 5, 2026: Lite is $39.99/month, billed $479.88 yearly. Standard is billed $900 yearly and Professional $1200 yearly. Paid every three months, they are $49.99, $99.99 and $119.99 per month. The pricing page shows yearly and quarterly billing. Those prices include data and rent estimates, plus rental comps on Standard and Professional, that DealLitmus's $39 one-time Pro plan does not have, so they are not like-for-like. Its separate Manage and API products are not compared here.

Mashvisor's pricing page says "We do not offer refunds." Its help center says refunds are issued only in exceptional cases and that Find & Invest subscriptions do not currently include a free trial. There is no contract, you can cancel online at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period, creating an account is free, and it advertises a free Airbnb calculator.

Consider DealLitmus if you already have the deal and its numbers. Try the free rental property calculator first, and check Mashvisor's pricing page before you decide.
