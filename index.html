Questions

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. One payment unlocks the plan on the devices you use. There is nothing to renew or cancel.

Where are my deals stored?

In your browser’s storage, on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Back up to save your deals and settings to a file, and Restore to move them to another device.

How do I unlock Pro after buying?

Your receipt email contains a license key. Open the app, click Upgrade, paste the key and press Activate. It takes about ten seconds.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Enter the same key on each device first. Deals don’t sync between devices automatically, so then use Back up on one device and Restore on the other to carry them over. Restore replaces the deals already on that device. The key comes first because on the free plan a restore keeps only the first 3 deals in the file. It tells you so before it replaces anything.

How are the numbers calculated?

With the standard formulas lenders and investors use: amortizing loan math, net operating income, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, debt service coverage ratio, and internal rate of return on yearly cash flows including sale proceeds. All figures are pre-tax, and every assumption is visible and editable.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet or desktop, with nothing to install.

What is the refund policy?

If it isn’t for you, reply to your receipt email within 14 days for a full refund.

Is this financial advice?

No. DealLitmus is a calculator. Results are estimates based on the assumptions you enter. Verify everything independently before you make an offer.
